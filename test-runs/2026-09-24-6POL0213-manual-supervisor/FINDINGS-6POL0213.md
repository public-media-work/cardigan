## 1. Bottom line

The job completed, but only after supervisor intervention at two points prod cannot make on its own. Left to prod's code, this job would have paused three separate times, each with a message that misdescribes the actual problem:

| Step | What prod would do | Actual cause |
|---|---|---|
| First QA gate | `[qa_review]` "the formatter flagged items it can't verify… Verify media_id + proper-noun spelling" | Chunk-0 prompt made Sonnet omit the header/Status; validator flag text contained the words "review notes"; substring match routed a model-fixable flag to a human (QA-1) |
| Escalation (if QA-1 had not fired) | `[qa_fail] QA failed after escalation` | Opus added review notes, `needs_review`, and `Speaker 1/2` labels at the chunk seam; contract markers make every formatter flag non-fixable; retry carried no feedback (RETRY-1) |
| Single-call path on a modern model (#409 + roster) | `[truncation]` + blocking seam gap | Reasoning tokens consumed 77% of `max_tokens` (LLM-1) |

What produced a passing job: single-call formatter on Sonnet 5 with reasoning disabled (finish `stop`, 100.3% coverage, header + Status present, no review notes), SEO on Sonnet instead of Haiku (Haiku cannot count characters), and the default Haiku validator. Timestamp passed untouched on the first try.

The prod backlog (jobs 29–48, all paused) matches these mechanisms closely: "nearly all `[qa_review]` citing review notes" is QA-1 + VAL-1; job 45's "Speaker 1 / Speaker 2" is the escalation outcome reproduced here; job 44's "status marked draft" is VAL-2; job 47's "section 1 of 2" header leak and this run's missing header are two faces of the same chunk-wrapper bug (FMT-1); job 48's truncation is #403, now with the reasoning-token dimension added.

## 4. Fix production or move on? A cost/benefit read

**Corrected the same day, after re-routing prod's 27 paused verdicts deterministically (GATE-1).** The four serial fixes below are real, cheap, and make *new* jobs like this one complete. They do **not** release the existing backlog, and they would not have prevented most of it: 25 of 27 paused jobs carry `Status: needs_review` and a review-notes block that the formatter wrote on purpose, and the gate pauses on that by design.

1. QA-1 (one list + one routing rule in `escalation.py`) — fixes this run's misroute, moves 0 backlog jobs
2. VAL-1 + VAL-2 (two sentences in `validator.md`) — stops two false positives, moves 0 backlog jobs
3. FMT-1 (two sentences in the chunk prompts, or a deterministic header/footer in the merge)
4. LLM-1 (already shipped by the peer on #407/#409, awaiting review/deploy)

With those four and SEO on Sonnet, a clean job completes on prod's own code paths without a supervisor. RETRY-1 makes retries useful again and is a small change in the same function as FMT-1. That is roughly a day of work plus review.

**Mark's ruling (2026-09-24):** the paused jobs are performance data only; their episodes already published, so the artifacts have no remaining use. Nobody should build a bridge to release them. GATE-1 stays open for *new* jobs: whether formatter uncertainty pauses a job or rides along as an item flag is the item-model thesis on the wayfinder map, and is v-next work.

What argues for v-next is different: SEO-2/SEO-3 (deterministic enforcement and full-transcript grounding), ATTR-1 (attribution needs a real signal), and the general pattern that prompts describe checks the pipeline does not run. Those are design choices, not bugs, and they are where the "plow forward" instinct is right.

Recommendation: ship the four hotfixes (plus RETRY-1 and the SEO-on-Sonnet config change) to the current deployment, because v-next inherits every one of them anyway, and let everything else land in v-next. Do not spend prod effort on SEO-3, ATTR-1, or the style-engine post-stage.
